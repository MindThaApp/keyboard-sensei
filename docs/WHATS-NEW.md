# What's new in Music Portal

## Music Portal 1.0

Midivory is now **Music Portal**. It is the same app and the same account, with
more to learn.

### New

- **Guitar and violin lessons**, from first steps up to the early intermediate
  level. Guitar has staff and tab together, chord diagrams and an on-screen
  fretboard. Violin has fingering, positions and bowing marks and an on-screen
  fingerboard.
- **Lessons that listen.** Play your own instrument into the microphone and get
  graded on notes and timing. On violin you also see how many cents sharp or flat
  each note was. Guitar chords are marked right or wrong with a hint about what
  the app heard.
- **Microphone piano.** Play single-line melodies on an acoustic piano and get
  graded without a MIDI keyboard.
- **A chromatic tuner** with guitar, violin and piano presets, an adjustable
  reference pitch (A4 from 415 to 466 Hz), a capo setting, custom tunings and a
  reference tone. It works offline, with no account.
- **Piano check.** Find out which notes on an acoustic piano are out of tune, how
  far, and whether the whole piano has drifted flat.
- **"What do you play?"** Choose piano, guitar or violin. Learn, Today and
  practice follow your choice, and you can switch any time.
- **Music Portal for Windows**, from the Microsoft Store, with USB MIDI
  keyboards and the microphone.
- **A new name and look**, including the app name, icon and web page title.

### Privacy

Microphone sound is analysed on your device. It is never recorded and never
uploaded. The privacy policy has a new Microphone section.

### Still the same

Your account, progress, streaks, settings and sign-in carry over from Midivory.
Everything piano learners could use before is still free for existing users.

### Coming later

- More guitar lessons (melodies in first position, fingerstyle, barre chords,
  pentatonic and blues) and more violin lessons (two-octave scales, third
  position, vibrato and double stops).
- A guided piano tuning aid, with safety guidance. Piano check only measures for
  now.
- Bluetooth MIDI keyboards on Windows.
- Grading of double stops on violin and of which string a guitar note was played
  on.
- Offline lessons.
