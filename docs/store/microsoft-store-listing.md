## Description

Music Portal is a friendly place to learn an instrument. Pick piano, guitar or
violin, follow short step-by-step lessons, and get feedback on what you play.
It was called Midivory before, and if you already use it, your progress and
settings carry over.

**Lessons that listen**
Play your own instrument and Music Portal listens through your microphone. It
checks your notes, your timing and, on violin, how close each note is to being
in tune. Guitar chords are marked as right or wrong, with a hint about what it
heard. Everything the microphone hears is analysed on your PC and thrown away.
Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

**Piano, guitar and violin**
- Piano: play with a USB MIDI keyboard, or use the on-screen keyboard. If you
  have an acoustic piano, microphone mode grades single-line melodies.
- Guitar: notes shown as staff and tab, chord diagrams, and an on-screen
  fretboard.
- Violin: fingering, positions and bowing marks, and an on-screen fingerboard.
- At launch, guitar and violin lessons run from first steps through to the
  early-intermediate level. More lessons are coming later.

**A tuner you can use straight away**
The chromatic tuner works without an account and without an internet
connection. It has presets for standard guitar tuning, Drop D, DADGAD, open
tunings and more, a violin preset, and a piano mode. You can set your own
reference pitch (A4 from 415 to 466 Hz) and save custom tunings.

**Piano check**
Not sure whether your acoustic piano needs tuning? Play the notes the app asks
for and it shows which are sharp or flat, how far, and whether the whole piano
has drifted down in pitch. You get a plain-language verdict to help you decide
whether to call a piano technician. It measures only. It does not teach you to
tune. A guided tuning aid is coming later.

**Keep going every day**
Daily challenges, quizzes, streaks and experience points keep practice
regular. One account works on the web, on Android and on Windows, and your
progress follows you.

**MIDI keyboards**
Connect a USB MIDI keyboard and play along. Bluetooth MIDI keyboards are
coming later on Windows.

**Free and paid**
The tuner and the first lessons for each instrument are free. Some of the
later lessons and extra tools are part of an optional upgrade. People who
already use Music Portal keep everything they have today.

**Your privacy**
Microphone sound is processed on your device and never stored or sent
anywhere. Read the privacy policy at the link below. Questions? Write to
mindthaapp@gmail.com.

## Features (one line each)

- Lessons for piano, guitar and violin
- Microphone grading: notes, timing and violin intonation
- Guitar chord checking with hints on what it heard
- Staff and tab notation, chord diagrams and fingering marks
- Chromatic tuner with guitar, violin and piano presets
- Adjustable reference pitch and custom tunings
- Piano check: see which notes on an acoustic piano are out of tune
- USB MIDI keyboard support
- On-screen piano, fretboard and fingerboard
- Daily challenges, quizzes, streaks and experience points
- Tuner works offline with no account needed
- Microphone sound stays on your device and is never uploaded
