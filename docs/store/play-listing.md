## Full description (4,000 characters or fewer)

Music Portal (formerly Midivory) helps you learn piano, guitar and violin with
short, friendly lessons that listen to you play.

LESSONS THAT LISTEN
Play your real instrument and Music Portal listens through your microphone. It
checks your notes and your timing. On violin it also shows how close each note
is to being in tune. For guitar, chords are marked right or wrong, with a hint
about what it heard. Everything the microphone picks up is analysed on your
phone and thrown away. Nothing is recorded and no audio is uploaded.

PIANO, GUITAR AND VIOLIN
- Piano: use a USB or Bluetooth MIDI keyboard, the on-screen keyboard, or your
  acoustic piano with the microphone (single-line melodies).
- Guitar: staff and tab together, chord diagrams and an on-screen fretboard.
- Violin: fingering, positions and bowing marks, and an on-screen fingerboard.
- Guitar and violin lessons run from first steps to the early intermediate
  level at launch. More are coming later.

A FREE TUNER
The chromatic tuner works with no account and no internet connection. Choose a
guitar preset (standard, Drop D, DADGAD, open tunings and more), the violin
preset or piano mode. Set your own reference pitch (A4 from 415 to 466 Hz) and
save custom tunings.

PIANO CHECK
Wondering whether your acoustic piano needs a tuner? Play the notes the app
asks for and see which are sharp or flat, by how much, and whether the whole
piano has drifted flat. A plain verdict helps you decide whether to call a
piano technician. It measures only; it does not teach tuning. A guided tuning
aid is coming later.

PRACTISE A LITTLE EVERY DAY
Daily challenges, quizzes, streaks and experience points keep you going. One
account works on the web, on Android and on Windows.

FREE AND PAID
The tuner and the first lessons for each instrument are free. Some later
lessons and extra tools are part of an optional upgrade. If you already use the
app, everything you have today stays yours.

PRIVACY
Music Portal asks for microphone access only when you open a lesson, the tuner
or Piano check, and explains why first. If you say no, everything that uses a
MIDI keyboard or the on-screen instruments still works.
