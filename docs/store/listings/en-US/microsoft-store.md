## Short description

Learn piano, guitar and violin with lessons that listen to you, plus a free tuner and a check for your acoustic piano.

## Description

Music Portal helps you learn piano, guitar and violin with short, friendly lessons that listen to you play. It was called Midivory before; your account and progress carry over.

**Lessons that listen**
Play your real instrument and Music Portal listens through your microphone. It checks your notes and your timing. On violin it also shows how close each note is to being in tune. For guitar, chords are marked right or wrong, with a hint about what it heard. Everything the microphone picks up is analysed on your PC and thrown away. Nothing is recorded and no audio is uploaded.

**Piano, guitar and violin**
- Piano: use a USB MIDI keyboard or the on-screen keyboard. With an acoustic piano, microphone mode grades single-line melodies. Bluetooth MIDI keyboards are coming later on Windows.
- Guitar: notes shown as staff and tab, chord diagrams and an on-screen fretboard.
- Violin: fingering, positions and bowing marks, and an on-screen fingerboard.
- At launch, guitar and violin lessons run from first steps to the early intermediate level. More are coming later.

**A free tuner**
The chromatic tuner works with no account and no internet connection. Choose a guitar preset (standard, Drop D, DADGAD, open tunings and more), the violin preset or piano mode. Set your own reference pitch (A4 from 415 to 466 Hz) and save custom tunings.

**Piano check**
Wondering whether your acoustic piano needs a tuner? Play the notes the app asks for and see which are sharp or flat, by how much, and whether the whole piano has drifted flat. A plain verdict helps you decide whether to call a piano technician. It measures only; it does not teach tuning. A guided tuning aid is coming later.

**Practise a little every day**
Daily challenges, quizzes, streaks and experience points keep you going. One account works on the web, on Android and on Windows.

**Free and paid**
The tuner and the first lessons for each instrument are free. Some later lessons and extra tools are part of an optional upgrade. If you already use the app, everything you have today stays yours.

**Privacy**
Music Portal asks for microphone access only when you open a lesson, the tuner or Piano check, and explains why first. If you say no, everything that uses a MIDI keyboard or the on-screen instruments still works.

Questions or ideas? Use Send feedback in the app.

## Features (one line each)

- Lessons for piano, guitar and violin
- Microphone grading: notes, timing and violin intonation
- Guitar chord checking with hints on what it heard
- Staff and tab notation, chord diagrams and fingering marks
- Chromatic tuner with guitar, violin and piano presets
- Adjustable reference pitch and custom tunings
- Piano check: see which notes on an acoustic piano are out of tune
- On-screen piano, fretboard and fingerboard
- Daily challenges, quizzes, streaks and experience points
- USB MIDI keyboard support
- Microphone sound stays on your device and is never uploaded
